# Cricket-IPL-Excel-Dashboard
An Excel dashboard for IPL Match 2016 to 2026 records
Click any Year in the slicer and all KPIs and charts together.

## What it shows
- Match Wins By Teams Bat First & Field Since 2016 - 2026
- Toss Decision Based Winning %
- Top 10 Venues
- Title Winner
- Top 10 Men of the Match  Award Winner

## Tools Used
MS Excel , Pivot Table, Pivot Charts , Slicer
